# Hossein Shahsavari

**Bioinformatician | Research + Engineering**

*Bridging Biology and Engineering through Bioinformatics.*

I am a bioinformatician with an undergraduate background in biology and graduate studies in engineering. I work at the intersection of biological questions, computational methods, and reliable scientific software.

My interests include **computational systems biology**, **RNA-seq**, **genome-scale metabolic modeling**, and **multi-omics integration**. I value transparent methods, reproducible analysis, and careful interpretation of evidence.

## Research interests

- **Systems biology:** constraint-based modeling, metabolic networks, and context-specific models
- **Cancer bioinformatics:** transcriptomic analysis and cancer metabolism
- **Multi-omics integration:** connecting molecular measurements to mechanistic models
- **Reproducible research:** traceable data, validated workflows, and clearly documented limitations
